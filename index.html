How to read the chart

The diagonal steps show each level and its personal discount: 35% at Assistant Supervisor up to 48% at Manager. The figures to the right of a step show what a higher level earns on volume from team members at that step. For example, a Manager earns 13% on an Assistant Supervisor’s volume (48% − 35%), and a Supervisor earns 3% (38% − 35%).

CC stands for Case Credits, Forever’s measure of sales volume. For full details and current rules, ask Sudha Shree or check Forever Living’s official marketing plan.
